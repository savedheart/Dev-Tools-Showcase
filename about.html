About DevTools

DevTools is a collection of simple and useful online tools designed to make everyday development tasks easier.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide developers with fast, accessible and easy-to-use utilities in one place. No installation or registration required.

What We Offer

  • JSON formatting and validation
  • UUID generation
  • Base64 encoding and decoding
  • Regular expression testing
  • Unix timestamp conversion
  • Cryptographic hash generation

Technologies

This project is built with HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, EJS templates and Vite.
